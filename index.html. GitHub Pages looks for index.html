About

Comrd Abubakar Alhaji Abdullahi, popularly known as A. Dabbagi, is a Community Health student leader at Niger State School of Health Technology, Minna.

For the 2026/2027 academic session, he serves as Secretary General of ACHSN. The role supports the association through communication, record keeping, meeting coordination, and administrative support.

His leadership profile emphasizes teamwork, accountability, effective communication, student representation, and the development of Community Health students.
